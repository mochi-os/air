moon-day.png and moon-night.png are the moon's near side as seen from Midway at the game's
two fixed moments, baked by tools/moon.py from the colour map of NASA's CGI Moon Kit
(https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720), itself from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter camera's
Hapke-normalised WAC mosaic. The geometry is from JPL Horizons.

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio
